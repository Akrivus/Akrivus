MobiMenu is an open-source solution for multimedia display units. It's been used for on-site digital signage such as restaurant and coffeeshop menus. While initially designed for the Raspberry Pi 4, it should work with most Unix-based operating systems with framebuffer devices.
##### Stack
Ruby, Sinatra, nginx
##### Objective
- Decode a raster image and write it to a framebuffer.
- Add a simple web service for updating the image and recoding.
- Add a discoverability system for ease of use.
##### Implementation
- Experimentation with an RPi 4 on my own monitor displays.
- Early morning live testing of web integration using Sinatra.
- Further tested of mDNS protocol for secure, automatic discovery.
##### Impact & Conclusion
- I saved my client 60% on licensing fees that year with a free solution, promoting further cost savings in perpetuity compared to other options.
- The next year every TV on the market had a photo mode, making me a trendsetter.
