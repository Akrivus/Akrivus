Syntreno, a seed-stage startup, aimed to revolutionize soft skills training by leveraging advanced AI technologies. The company envisioned a product that could simulate real-life interactions using GPT-4-controlled virtual characters, providing users with immersive, voice-interactive training experiences. As the Lead Software Engineer, I was tasked with developing this flagship product, overcoming the technical challenges of integrating AI with Unity, and navigating the complexities of bringing such an innovative solution to market.
##### Duration
August 2023 - August 2024
##### Stack
C#, Unity, Python, Ruby, Sinatra, Heroku
##### Duties
- Developed Syntreno's flagship product in Unity.
- Developed a JSON API in Ruby for license management and API access.
- Collaborated with C-suite to conceptualize and align development with business objectives.
##### Results
- Developed a training simulation in Unity that used GPT-4 to control virtual actors in a scene, using Whisper and TTS to provide an immersive, voice-interactive experience.
- Developed an audio processing library in JavaScript for low-latency talking animations.
- Thwarted abuse of cloud resources with security features such as Rack::Attack.

By overcoming the challenges of integrating AI with Unity, my work at Syntreno created a groundbreaking solution that set the stage for future growth. This experience reinforces my belief in the power of intuitive, user-centered design and the importance of strategic thinking in bringing cutting-edge, yet immature, technologies to market.
