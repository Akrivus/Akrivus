ChatWS (Chat-WebSocket) was a project to familiarize me with WebSocket. I'd previously built socket.io clients in Ruby, but never WebSocket client/servers in full-stack.
##### Stack
Ruby, Sinatra, WebSockets
##### Objective
- Build a simple chat room powered by WebSockets.
##### Implementation
- Installed sinatra-websocket gem.
- Wrote JavaScript to receive WebSocket messages and append a div.
- Used config.ru for quick deployment to Heroku.
- Profit.
##### Impact & Conclusion
- I made a chat app in the span of a day.
- I learned how to use Bundler.
- I added a Markdown renderer a year later.
